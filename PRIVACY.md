# PennyLog Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

**PennyLog does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or financial information.** It has no servers and no user accounts.

## What stays on your device
- The expenses, income, budgets and notes you enter, and any statement files you import, are processed and stored **only on your device**, in the app's local storage.
- Statement files you choose (CSV or Excel) are read on your device and are never uploaded.
- The app does not connect to your bank, your email or any payment app.

## What we don't use
No advertising, analytics, crash reporting, tracking or third-party services of any kind. The app works fully offline.

## Files you export
When you export a CSV or a backup, the file is created on your device and you choose where it goes (Files, Drive, another app). Once it leaves PennyLog it is covered by that app's or service's own policies.

## Deleting your data
Use **Settings → Erase all data**, or uninstall the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else, so this removes everything.

## Changes
If this policy ever changes, the updated version and date will appear here and at `www/privacy.html`.
